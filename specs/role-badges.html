Role badges

A small mark after a person's name that says what they do in a space. Every space starts with sixteen defaults and can make its own, using a built-in glyph or an uploaded image. Shape tells you who gave the role, colour comes from the six identity colours, and the glyph is always ink.

Roles without a badge keep the dot. Tags sort staff first, then community, then system.
Sizes16 in chat and tags, 20 in lists, 32 in role settings, 64 in the editor. Glyph strokes thicken at small sizes so they stay readable at 16.
ColourSix identity colours plus white and grey. The glyph is always ink #0a0a0a, which stays above 7:1 on every fill.
LimitsUp to 50 badged roles per space. Spined shapes are kept for staff, so a custom Community role can't pass as a moderator.
FederationBadges travel as tier, colour and glyph name, plus an image URL for uploads. Other fediverse apps show the role name as text.
