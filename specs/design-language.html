Pufferblow Design language · v1
A quiet room full of loud fish.

The client chrome is black, grey and white. Colour belongs to people and spaces: avatars, server icons, banners and role tags. Federation is always visible, and the puffer shows up in the small moments: reactions, typing, loading, mentions.

01 Chrome is mono, people are colour UI surfaces never use the identity palette. If something is coloured, it belongs to a person or a space.
02 Everyone has a home Remote people and spaces always show their instance. Local ones don't, so the difference reads at a glance.
03 Playful at the edges Layouts stay calm and dense. Motion and character live in feedback: puffing, bubbling, bristling.

Avatar, frame (as a thin ring) and name style follow you into lists and chat. Theme, banner and sections stay on the card and full profile, so the rest of the client stays calm.
Readability guardThemes mix the chosen colour into the dark card at 16–24% max, so white text always stays above 4.5:1. Users pick the colour, never the text colour.
UploadsAvatar 512 × 512, banner 960 × 384 (5:2). PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP up to 8 MB. Animated images play on hover only.
LimitsName 32 · pronouns 40 · status 60 · bio 190 characters. Up to 4 sections, reorderable by drag in settings.
FederationProfile settings travel as plain fields on the actor. Other Pufferblow instances render the full card; other fediverse apps get the avatar, banner, name and bio.
Viewer contextMutual spaces, mutual friends and roles are worked out for whoever opens the card, so they can't be edited. Roles show for the space you're in, up to five, then +N. The message box sends a DM without leaving the channel.
Status and presenceCustom status floats beside the avatar as a thought bubble, up to 60 characters. The presence dot becomes a phone when the person is only active on mobile.
