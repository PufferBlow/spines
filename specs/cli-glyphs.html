Pufferblow · CLI and TUI

Glyph marks for the terminal

The puffer mark rebuilt from text characters, sized in terminal cells. Colour marks are cream with brass eyes and mouth, in truecolor ANSI with a 256-colour fallback. Mono marks take the terminal's foreground colour, with the eyes and mouth left as holes. Every mark is mirrored from its left half so it stays symmetric.
